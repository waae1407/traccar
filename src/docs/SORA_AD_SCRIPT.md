# Contactless360 — Sora AI Ad Script

## VIDEO SPEC
- **Length:** 60 seconds (Sora max)
- **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) — primary
- **Alt:** 16:9 cutdown for YouTube/website hero
- **Tone:** Cinematic, high-energy, premium tech, emotional hook
- **Music:** Dark bass-heavy build → explosive drop at 0:15 → cinematic strings under emotion beats
- **Color grade:** Deep navy night → magenta-pink neon accents → warm golden hero shots

## SCENE-BY-SCENE SCRIPT

### SCENE 1 — THE HOOK (0:00–0:04)
**VISUAL:** Black screen. A single car key spins in slow motion under a spotlight. It dissolves into a phone.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Your car keys are obsolete."
**VO (deep, confident):** "What if your car… could protect itself?"
**SOUND:** Deep bass hit. Key clink echoes.

### SCENE 2 — THE PROBLEM (0:04–0:08)
**VISUAL:** Quick cuts — a parking lot at night, empty. A worried owner checking their phone. A stolen car speeding away (drone shot).
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Every 26 seconds, a car is stolen in America."
**VO:** "Every 26 seconds. Gone. No trace. No recovery."
**SOUND:** Heartbeat pulse. Siren wail in distance.

### SCENE 3 — THE REVEAL (0:08–0:12)
**VISUAL:** The Contactless360 device materializes from particles, rotating hero shot on pure black, neon magenta rim light. Glowing.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Meet Contactless360."
**VO:** "Meet Contactless360. One device. Total control."
**SOUND:** Tech shimmer. Music builds.

### SCENE 4 — LIVE GPS & TRIP HISTORY (0:12–0:16)
**VISUAL:** Split screen — left: car driving on highway at night. Right: phone screen showing a live glowing map with the car moving in real-time, route line trailing behind in magenta.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Live GPS. 24/7."
**VO:** "Track it live. Replay every trip. Every turn. Every stop."
**SOUND:** Map ping. Engine hum.

### SCENE 5 — ANTI-THEFT & IMMOBILIZER (0:16–0:20)
**VISUAL:** Thief opens car door. Phone buzzes. Owner taps "DISABLE STARTER." The car's engine light flashes red — dead. Thief confused, slams steering wheel.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Stolen? Kill the engine. Instantly."
**VO:** "Stolen? One tap. Engine dead. Stuck. Done."
**SOUND:** Alarm pulse. Engine cut sound.

### SCENE 6 — KEYLESS ENTRY & CONTACTLESS RENTALS (0:20–0:25)
**VISUAL:** Renter walks up to car, phone in pocket. Doors auto-unlock with a soft glow. They drive off. Owner watches payment notification pop up on phone.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "No keys. No meetups. Just income."
**VO:** "Rent it out. Keyless. Contactless. While you sleep."
**SOUND:** Soft unlock chime. Cash register ding.

### SCENE 7 — VEHICLE FINDER (0:25–0:28)
**VISUAL:** Massive dark parking garage. Owner taps "FIND" on phone. Car horn honks, headlights flash in the distance, illuminating the path.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Find it in any lot."
**VO:** "Lost in a lot? Flash. Honk. Found."
**SOUND:** Horn honk echo in garage.

### SCENE 8 — SMART ALERTS & BATTERY HEALTH (0:28–0:33)
**VISUAL:** Phone screen fills with alerts stacking: "🔋 Low Battery — 11.2V" "💨 Heavy Smoke Detected" "📍 Movement Alert" "⚠️ Tamper Detected." Then a battery health graph showing projected hours-to-dead countdown.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "It warns you BEFORE it dies."
**VO:** "Battery dying. Smoke. Movement. Tampering. It tells you first. Hours before it's dead."
**SOUND:** Notification chimes stacking. Tension build.

### SCENE 9 — BACKUP BATTERY REVEAL (0:33–0:37)
**VISUAL:** A hand cuts the car's battery cable (dramatic). The device's internal backup battery glows green — still tracking on the map.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Power cut? Still tracking. 2 hours."
**VO:** "They cut the power? Internal backup keeps tracking for two hours."
**SOUND:** Cable cut. Then steady green pulse.

### SCENE 10 — ACCIDENT & EMERGENCY SOS (0:37–0:43)
**VISUAL:** Car swerves, impact (suggested, not graphic). Screen flashes red: "ACCIDENT DETECTED." Emergency contacts' phones light up simultaneously — SMS, email, push. GPS location shared.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Crash? It calls for help. Automatically."
**VO:** "Crash detected. Emergency contacts alerted instantly. GPS shared. Help on the way."
**SOUND:** Impact thud (muted). Then rapid alert cascade. Emotional strings swell.

### SCENE 11 — SAFE DRIVING MONITOR (0:43–0:47)
**VISUAL:** Speedometer needle redlines. Map shows erratic route with hard cornering markers. Phone alert: "⚠️ Reckless Driving Detected."
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "It watches HOW they drive."
**VO:** "Drag racing. Hard cornering. Sudden stops. You'll know."
**SOUND:** Tire screech. Rapid notification.

### SCENE 12 — THE EMOTIONAL TURN (0:47–0:52)
**VISUAL:** Slow-motion: a parent watching their teen drive away for the first time. They look at their phone — the car is safe, tracked, protected. They exhale. Smile.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Because what you love is worth protecting."
**VO (softer, emotional):** "Because what you love… is worth protecting."
**SOUND:** Music drops to just piano. Emotional swell.

### SCENE 13 — THE MONTAGE (0:52–0:56)
**VISUAL:** Rapid-fire 1-second cuts of every feature in action — lock, unlock, track, alert, SOS, finder, immobilizer. Each cut synced to a beat.
**TEXT OVERLAY:** (rapid) "Track. Lock. Find. Protect. Alert. Monitor. Control."
**VO:** "Track. Lock. Find. Protect. This is everything."
**SOUND:** Beat drops hard. Each cut hits on the beat.

### SCENE 14 — THE CALL TO ACTION (0:56–0:60)

## POST-PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Hook test:** The first 3 seconds must stop the scroll. The "keys are obsolete" line + key dissolving into phone is the scroll-stopper.
- **Emotional anchor:** Scene 12 (parent + teen) is the shareable moment that drives saves and shares — protect this beat.
- **Feature density:** 7 features in 60 seconds is aggressive. If Sora struggles, prioritize: Immobilizer (Scene 5), Keyless Rental (Scene 6), Accident SOS (Scene 10), Backup Battery (Scene 9) — these are the most novel and viral.
- **CTA:** End on the 30-day free trial — it's the lowest-friction entry point and removes purchase hesitation.
- **Captions:** Burn in all text overlays — 80% of social viewers watch muted.
- **Hashtags for posting:** #Contactless360 #CarSecurity #GPS #AntiTheft #Keyless #CarTech #VehicleProtection #FleetManagement #SmartCar
